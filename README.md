## About

I am an undergraduate at **Shanghai Jiao Tong University** and a research intern at **XYZL**. I am interested in how language models become reliable, long-running agents: how they learn from executable feedback, manage repository-scale context, and keep improving without forgetting what they already know.

Right now I am working on:

- repository-level coding agents and verifiable evaluation;
- reinforcement learning for long-horizon agent behavior;
- continual learning and model adaptation;
- efficient inference and practical LLM systems.

I also enjoy chess, reading, and building small tools that make complicated things easier to use.
